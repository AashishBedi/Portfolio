Featured Projects
EduVerse – Educational AI Assistant
Oct 2025 - Mar 2026

Designed an intent-aware hybrid RAG architecture routing university queries to deterministic SQL, vector-based semantic retrieval, or both for hybrid queries. Built an intent classifier using unigram/bigram signal vocabularies and regex patterns, with section-aware chunking, cosine-similarity retrieval, and collection-specific thresholds. Built and manually validated a 150-query benchmark, achieving 90.8% end-to-end accuracy, 93.4% retrieval accuracy, and a 5.0% hallucination rate — outperforming LLM-only, SQL-only, and vector-only RAG baselines. Findings contributed to a research paper on reliable university information access.
