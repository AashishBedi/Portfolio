01CAPSTONE & RESEARCHOCT 2025 – MAR 2026

EduVerse

Educational Data Platform

A university information assistant that routes factual questions to SQL and document questions to semantic retrieval. Designed around a simple principle: exact university facts need exact data.

PythonSQL / SQLiteChromaDBSentence TransformersFastAPI
Architecture & evaluation

Rule-based intent routing uses unigram/bigram signal vocabularies and regex patterns. Section-aware chunks, cosine similarity and collection-specific thresholds select document context; structured queries use SQL. Hybrid questions use both. Responses are constrained to retrieved context, with a fallback when relevant evidence is missing.

Evaluated on a hand-built, manually checked 150-query benchmark spanning ordinary, mixed-intent and edge-case questions. The reported results contributed to a research paper on reliable university information access.
